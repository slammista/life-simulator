# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Mobile-first casual players aged 15–35. They pick up the game in short sessions (5–15 min) during commutes, breaks, and idle time. Familiar with BitLife and similar life-sim games. Expect instant narrative feedback from every tap. Primary device: smartphone (portrait). Secondary: desktop in a mobile-sized window.

## Product Purpose

LifeSimulator2D is a choice-driven life simulation game inspired by BitLife. Players navigate a procedurally-generated life from birth to death, making decisions across career, relationships, health, crime, and more. The game loop is: advance a year → receive an event → choose a response → see consequences → repeat. Monetized through ad-rewarded bonuses and Stripe IAP (gems, God Mode). Success is measured by session length, daily retention, and conversion rate.

## Brand Personality

Playful, punchy, irreverent. Three words: **dramatic, reactive, addictive**.  
Emotional goals: the player should feel the pull of "just one more year", dread when stats drop, and dopamine-hit euphoria when rare events land. The interface is the game, not a frame around the game.

## Anti-references

- **SaaS dashboards** (Linear, Notion, Vercel) — clean, task-oriented, restrained typography, card-grid navigation
- **Fintech apps** (Revolut, N26) — data-first KPI widgets, metric bars, wallet chips
- **Productivity tools** (Todoist, Things) — checklist-based "suggested actions", goal-tracking panels
- **Admin panels** — breadcrumb back bars, sub-tab pill navs, uppercase section labels with letter-spacing

The interface must feel like a mobile game, not a B2B product. No card-grid quick actions. No tracked-uppercase section headers. No "recommended actions" panels that look like CRM task items.

## Design Principles

1. **Event first** — The active event and its choices are the core loop. They dominate the screen. Everything else is secondary.
2. **Reaction over dashboard** — Feedback is immediate, emotional, and dramatic. A stat change is an event, not a KPI update.
3. **Narrative above navigation** — Users live a life; they do not manage feature categories. Navigation serves story.
4. **Dopamine by design** — Rarity, color, sound, and motion reinforce reward loops. Every "+3 Felicità" should feel earned and visible.
5. **Game grammar, not SaaS grammar** — No uppercase eyebrow headers. No breadcrumb back bars. No productivity-style checklists as objectives. No fintech wallet chips as the primary money display.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA contrast targets. Haptics and audio as opt-in enhancements. Touch targets ≥ 44pt for mobile-first play. Reduced-motion alternative for all animations.
